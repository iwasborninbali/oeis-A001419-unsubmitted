# Prior art

Neal Madras, *A pattern theorem for lattice clusters*, Annals of Combinatorics 3 (1999), 357–384, [doi:10.1007/BF01608793](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01608793), gives the pattern theorem. Johann Peters records its holey-polyomino consequence in [A000105](https://oeis.org/A000105) (Jan 06 2024), as reported in the lab answer. John Mason's conjecture remains in [A001419](https://oeis.org/A001419), read 2026-11-13; the latter does not cite Madras. No new theorem is claimed.
